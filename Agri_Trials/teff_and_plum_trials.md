# AGRICULTURAL TRIALS - PAHOA BASELINE

## 1. Food Forest Guild & Clearing (Puna Site)
* **Objective:** Establishing foundational food forest guilds, soil enhancement, and expanding planting space.
* **Current Crops Active:** Kalo, beans, oleana (turmeric), garlic chives, greens, and emerging sprouts.
* **Methodology:** Manual clearing, rock-terraced bed preparation, multi-crop companion integration, and low-impact site management.
* **Status:** Active cultivation and land expansion underway.

## 2. Future Trials & Seed Bank
* **Status:** Teff grain and Natal plum propagation trials deferred to subsequent phases as primary focus remains on food forest guild establishment.
