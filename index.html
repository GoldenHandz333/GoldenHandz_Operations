Aloha and welcome to the foundation.

When you look at a piece of wild, overgrown land, what do you see? Some see heavy labor; some see chaos. At GoldenHandz Agri & Media LLC, operating out of Pahoa and Hilo, we see raw potential—a blank canvas ready to be restored, cultivated, and transformed into a living, breathing food forest.

The Work in Motion: Creating a Mini Food Forest

Our vision for GoldenHandz is the creation of a mini food forest. This process begins with establishing essential canopy and sub-canopy layers. We've introduced a foundational U'lu and established multiple Banana plants to provide critical initial biomass. Following this, our primary intercropping involves a robust crop of Taro supported by beans, which serve as a natural nitrogen component. To complete the system, we are using sweet potatoes as a robust cover crop, effectively managing the soil surface and suppressing competition while building structure.

Current Planting Guilds & Sprouting Understory
