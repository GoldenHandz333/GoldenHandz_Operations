Aloha and welcome to the foundation.

When you look at a piece of wild, overgrown land, what do you see? Some see heavy labor; some see chaos. At GoldenHandz Agri & Media LLC, operating out of Pahoa and Hilo, we see raw potential—a blank canvas ready to be restored, cultivated, and transformed into a living, breathing food forest.

Field Operations & Trial Plots

Here are the first operational photos from our site, documenting initial layouts and ongoing land restoration.

Our flagship food forest tree, established from seed. A young, healthy specimen showing vigorous new growth, surrounded by mulched material to retain moisture. This is the core of our regenerative plot.
A close-up of our experimental solar dehydrator setup. This passive system uses direct sunlight to dry produce, with the clear panels tilted for optimal angle. It's a key part of our value-added processing.
A dense, established row of diverse crops demonstrating polyculture. We are using intensive planting methods to maximize soil cover and biodiversity. This bed includes legumes, greens, and support species.
