# GOLDENHANDZ - OPERATIONAL SCOPE BOUNDARY

## 1. Pahoa Operations (Agricultural & Land Stewardship)
* **Core Focus:** Agricultural trials, small-scale teff grain cultivation, Natal plum (*Carissa macrocarpa*) propagation, and permaculture food forest design.
* **Service Boundary:** On-site consultations, land maintenance, and low-impact food forest integration. 
* **Important Delineation:** Yard maintenance, mowing, and consultation services are explicitly provided, while pre-existing permanent structural features (such as existing rock walls) remain part of the host terrain baseline.

## 2. Hilo Operations (Media & Administrative Infrastructure)
* **Core Focus:** Digital media production, repository version control, website/blog deployment, and trust administrative management.
* **Service Boundary:** Content creation, documentation, layout architecture, and commercial brand stewardship.

## 3. Structural Directive
*Maintaining clean boundaries between field-level agricultural/land work in Pahoa and media/administrative systems in Hilo ensures operational clarity and precise execution.*
